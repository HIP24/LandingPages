Questions

Good to know

Is the Sheikh a real scholar?

No. The Sheikh is an AI, guided to answer from the Quran and Sunnah. He can help you learn and reflect, but he is not a mufti and gives no fatwas. For serious religious matters, please ask a qualified scholar.

Which languages does he speak?

English, Arabic, Turkish, German, French, Spanish and Italian, in chat and in talks.
