# Sheikhify Landing Page

The website of Sheikhify, your personal Sheikh, in the app's night glass look.

## Files

- `index.html` – the landing page: the app's scene (SVG, day and night), the features with the app's screens, Free and Premium, questions
- `privacy.html` – Privacy Policy (linked from the app's Settings and the stores)
- `terms.html` – Terms of Use (linked from the App Store description)
- `delete-account.html` – how to delete an account (linked from Google Play)
- `style.css` – the shared glass styles; `site.js` – day/night switch, background sounds, Get the app, fade-ins
- `img/` – logo, store icons, `hero_night.svg`/`hero_day.svg` (the app's home scene as SVG, made by sheikhify's `design/store-screenshots/tools/hero_svg.py`), `screen_*.jpg` and `day/screen_*.jpg` (the app's screens by night and by day)
- `sound/` – the app's day and night background sounds
