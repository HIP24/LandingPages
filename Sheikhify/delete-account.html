Delete your account

Last updated: October 24, 2026

In the app

  1. Open Sheikhify and sign in.
  2. Open Settings (the gear on Home).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account Permanently.
  4. Confirm, and sign in once more if asked.

What is removed

Your sign-in, your email, name, photo, gender and the Sheikh's memory of you are removed right away, and you are signed out.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. It does not cancel a subscription: cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Without the app

If you cannot open the app, write to halilpamuk24@gmail.com from the email address of your account, and we will delete it for you.
